About NewsSort

How it works

  1. Clean. The title and description are joined and cleaned the same way the models were trained (HTML leftovers removed; heavier cleaning for the TF-IDF models, light cleaning for BERT).
  2. Predict. Logistic Regression and Random Forest read TF-IDF features. BERT reads word pieces with context. Each model returns a probability for World, Sports, Business and Sci/Tech.
  3. Flag. The top topic and confidence are shown. If the top two topics are close, if Business and Sci/Tech compete, or if the models disagree, the article is flagged for a human to check.

Limits

  • Only four broad topics. Works best on English headlines with a short description.
  • On the AG News test set, BERT misclassified 428 of 7,600 articles (about 1 in 18). Some labels in that data are ambiguous.
  • It assists a human editor; it does not replace one.
